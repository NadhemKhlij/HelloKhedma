        Who is responsible

        What we collect and why

        • Without an account: nothing is stored on our servers. Your applications, training progress, CV text and settings stay in your browser (local storage).
        • With an account: your email address and password (managed by Supabase Auth), the applications you track, your training progress and selected career path, to provide the synced service you asked for (performance of a contract).
        • Your CV never leaves your browser, even with an account. It is read and scored on your device only.
        • Job searches: the job title, place and country you search are sent to our search function and to the job providers (Adzuna, JSearch) to fetch offers. Identical searches are cached for 6 hours without any personal data.
        • Abuse protection: to limit searches per hour we keep a one-way hash of your IP address (guests) or your account id, for at most 24 hours (legitimate interest).
        • Error reports: if error monitoring is enabled, technical error details (page, browser, error message) are sent to Sentry to fix bugs (legitimate interest).

        Cookies and local storage

        HelloKhedma uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for your settings, guest data and, when signed in, your session. No consent banner is needed for these strictly necessary uses.

        Service providers

        • Supabase (database, authentication, server functions).
        • GitHub Pages (website hosting; their servers process your IP address to deliver the pages).
        • Adzuna and JSearch / RapidAPI (job offers, receive your search terms).
        • Google Fonts (font files) and cdnjs / Cloudflare (PDF reader, loaded only when you upload a PDF): they receive your IP address when your browser downloads these files.

        Some providers are located outside the EU; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

        How long we keep data

        Account data is kept until you delete your account. Search cache: 6 hours. Rate-limit hashes: 24 hours.
