        What we collect and why

        • Without an account: nothing is stored on our servers. Your applications, training progress, CV text and settings stay in your browser (local storage).
        • With an account: your email address and password (managed by Supabase Auth), the applications you track (including the name of the CV version you sent, never its content), your training progress and selected career path, to provide the synced service you asked for (performance of a contract).
        • Your CVs (every version) stay in your browser, even with an account: they are read and scored on your device only. The one exception is at your request: if, signed in, you click "Send and rewrite" (AI rewriting), the CV and that offer are sent to Claude (Anthropic) to produce the suggestions, after a notice shown every time. HelloKhedma does not store them (legal basis: your request, performance of the service).
        • Job searches: the job title, place and country you search are sent to our search function and to the job providers (Adzuna, JSearch) to fetch offers. Identical searches are cached for 6 hours without any personal data.
        • Abuse protection: to limit searches per hour and AI rewrites per day we keep a one-way hash of your IP address (guests) or your account id, for at most 24 hours (legitimate interest).
        • Error reports: if error monitoring is enabled, technical error details (page, browser, error message) are sent to Sentry to fix bugs (legitimate interest).

        Cookies and local storage

        HelloKhedma uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for your settings (including the interface language), guest data and, when signed in, your session. If you use the app offline or install it, your browser also keeps a copy of the app files and public course content on your device. No consent banner is needed for these strictly necessary uses.

        Service providers

        • Supabase (database, authentication, server functions).
        • Anthropic (Claude API, United States): receives your CV and one offer only when you ask for an AI rewrite, to produce the answer. See its privacy policy (anthropic.com).
        • GitHub Pages (website hosting; their servers process your IP address to deliver the pages).
        • Adzuna and JSearch / RapidAPI (job offers, receive your search terms).
        • Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and SmartRecruiters (companies' careers pages): when you follow a company, your browser loads its public job list directly from these services, which receive your IP address. The list of companies you follow stays in your browser.

        • Google Fonts (font files) and cdnjs / Cloudflare (PDF reader, loaded only when you upload a PDF): they receive your IP address when your browser downloads these files.

        Some providers are located outside the EU; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.
